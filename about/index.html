A little about me

Curiosity gets
the idea started.

I’m Alejandro, a software engineer based in Vancouver. I care about the whole experience: how a product feels, how it works, and how all the pieces fit together.

A builder, across disciplines.

My work spans native iOS engineering, product design, full-stack development, and AI. I’ve helped teams deliver consumer and enterprise apps, modernize existing systems, and navigate product pivots.

Through AZIO Labs, I also build independent products. Bento, Sticker Llama, Letterbox, and Poly Vita are different expressions of the same interest: taking an idea seriously enough to make it real.
