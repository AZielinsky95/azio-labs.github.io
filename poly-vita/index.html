I solo-developed Poly Vita in Unity and released it across iOS, Android, and Steam.

Players build pathways one tile at a time, navigating increasingly intricate puzzles without the pressure of a timer. The game combines spatial problem-solving, turn-based obstacles, and a quiet sense of exploration.
